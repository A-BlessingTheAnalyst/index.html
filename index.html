AI Academy Data Skills Certification Exam

Excel, Power BI, and SQL. 50 questions. 48 minutes. Read every rule below before you register.

This exam is monitored. Screen sharing, tab focus, fullscreen status, and copy or paste attempts are all logged. Please close every other tab, application, and notification before you begin.

Exam rules and regulations

  1. You have 48 minutes to answer all 50 questions. A countdown timer is visible at the top of the screen at all times. The exam submits automatically the moment the timer reaches zero.
  2. You get one attempt. Once you click Start Exam, the clock begins and cannot be paused.
  3. Before the exam begins, your browser will ask for permission to share your screen. You must share your entire screen for the full duration of the exam. Stopping screen sharing at any point is recorded as a violation.
  4. The exam runs in fullscreen. Exiting fullscreen, switching tabs, switching applications, or letting the window lose focus is recorded as a violation.
  5. Copying, cutting, and pasting are disabled for the duration of the exam. Right click and a number of keyboard shortcuts are also disabled.
  6. Repeated violations will end your exam early and flag your submission for instructor review.
  7. Use the Previous and Next buttons to move between questions. You may revisit and change any answer before you submit. A question grid at the bottom of the exam shows your progress.
  8. Each question has exactly one correct option. Answer options and question order are shuffled individually for every candidate, so do not expect the same question order or option order as anyone sitting near you.
  9. No external help, second devices, browser extensions, printed notes, or collaboration of any kind is permitted.
  10. When you submit, your score and a full activity log are sent to your instructor automatically. You will also see your own score on screen once you submit.
  11. By entering your details below and clicking Start Exam, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to comply with all of the rules above.
